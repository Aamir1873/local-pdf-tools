LOCAL PDF TOOLS v%%VERSION%%
Merge, split and organise PDFs without uploading them.

HOW TO USE
1. Double-click "Local PDF Tools.html". It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
2. Pick a tool: Merge, Split, Pages (reorder, rotate, delete) or Images to PDF.
3. Choose your files. The new PDF is saved to your Downloads folder.

It works without internet. To check, turn off Wi-Fi and try it.

KEEP IT ONE CLICK AWAY
- First move "Local PDF Tools.html" from Downloads to your Documents folder.
- Mac: drag the file to the right side of the Dock (next to the Trash), or Control-click > Make Alias and put the alias on your desktop.
- Windows: right-click the file > Show more options > Send to > Desktop (create shortcut).
- Any computer: open it and press Ctrl + D (Command-D on a Mac) to bookmark it in your browser's bookmarks bar.
- If it opens in the wrong app: right-click > Open with > your web browser.
The tool's "Keep it handy" section has the same steps with more detail.
